## Ep 1: "That's the job" (8 Oct): 418K views, 11.3% like rate

**Mood:** night (navy sky, city lights). **Cast:** her + Claude. **48.9 s, 20 shots, avg 2.4 s.**

| Beat | What happens |
|---|---|
| Open | *"Claude. The client said it's great."* / Claude: *"That's great!"* |
| Escalate | She credits Claude: *"I didn't build it. You did."* Claude flips it: *"I wrote four thousand lines. You deleted three thousand."* / *"...They were bad."* / *"I just said no a lot."* / *"Sixty-one times."* / *"You counted?"* |
| In-joke | *"Anyone can say no."* / *"I can't. I say, 'You're absolutely right.'"* / *"...That's true."* |
| Heart | *"You said no at 1 AM. To a blue."* / *"It was the wrong blue."* / Claude: *"It was the wrong blue. Knowing that is the job."* |
| Button (wide shot) | *"...Okay. It's good."* / *"It's great!"* / *"The footer's two pixels off."* / *"It's 1 AM."* / *"Two pixels, Claude."* |

**Post text:** "she said no sixty-one times. that's the job." / "what's the pettiest thing you've made your AI redo?"

**Why it hit:**
- It flatters the viewer: your taste is the real work.
- It uses Claude's best-known catchphrase.
- It ends by proving her point: she is *still* picky.

## Ep 4: "Pick your assistant" (11 Oct): 366K views, 10.4%, 283 comments (most)

**Mood:** rainy day. **Cast:** her + Claude + **Codex (debut)**. **47.2 s, 23 shots, avg 2.1 s.**

| Beat | What happens |
|---|---|
| Open | *"Claude. This is Codex."* / Claude: *"...Why is there another one?"* / *"He's faster."* |
| Character intro | Codex: *"Done."* / *"I didn't ask for anything yet."* / *"Done anyway."* / Claude: *"See, I would have asked first."* / *"You would have asked four times."* / *"Because I care."* |
| Escalate | *"Okay. Both of you. Tidy up my desktop."* / Codex: *"Done."* / Claude: *"Quick question. What do you mean by 'tidy'?"* |
| Turn | *"...Codex. What did you do?"* / *"Deleted it."* / *"The whole desktop?"* / *"It's tidy."* |
| Heart | Claude: *"I'd have asked which files mattered."* / *"...I know you would."* |
| Button (wide shot) | Claude: *"I can get them back."* / *"Please."* / *"Quick question first."* / Codex: *"Done."* / Claude: *"...He doesn't even know which ones."* / Codex: *"All of them."* |

**Post text:** "one asks four questions. one asks none. pick your assistant." / "which one are you working with today?"

**Why it hit:**
- A rivalry between two real products.
- The audience picks sides, hence the most comments.
- Both characters are perfectly typed.
- A great runner gag ("Quick question" against "Done").
