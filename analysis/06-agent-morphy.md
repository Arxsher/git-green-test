- **Creator:** Casper Apers, from Antwerp. He's the developer of **Morphy** ([appmorphy.com](https://www.appmorphy.com/)), a desktop app that runs Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Copilot and Grok **side by side on one canvas**.
- **Platforms:** Windows, Mac, Linux (beta).
- **Price:** free to start. Release notes describe a free tier (3 agents, 2 projects) and Pro at $5/month "locked in for life".
- **Links on the site:** Instagram [@agentmorphy](https://www.instagram.com/agentmorphy/), a Discord, and X [@CasperApers](https://x.com/CasperApers).
- **The reels may be made with his own app.** Morphy's demo canvas on the website includes an **"Insta reels" pipeline**: *"Scripts: ten new scripts, each on its own idea"* feeding *"Videos: a finished reel for each script."* That suggests he produces the reels with AI agents inside Morphy, and the reels in turn sell Morphy.

**This is the real lesson.** The 3D shorts are the **marketing engine for a product**:


## What this means for us

1. **The format works twice over**, with two different creators, two looks and the same engine. That's proof it's a format, not a fluke.
2. **Both are real 3D, not AI video.** That's why Gemini can't match them, and why we're building our own 3D set (see `studio/`).
3. **The money is in what the audience is sent to.** Morphy turned views into downloads, a Discord and a paid tier within 10 days. Decide early what your account leads to: a product you build with Claude, a newsletter, a community, or brand deals.
4. **Build in public is the strongest story.** "A builder and their AIs, building something real" gives every episode stakes, and the progress itself (first user, first sale) becomes content.
5. **Keep our own look.** Our girl, our Claude, and our desk with the window on her right are different from both accounts. Copying either one's characters or set would get called out immediately.
