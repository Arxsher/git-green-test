# Test episode 01: "Less angry"

A test of whether the format can be made with ChatGPT images, Gemini video clips (10 s each is fine), a voice tool and CapCut, all on a phone.

**Cast (original, not the @canwebuildthis characters)**
- **Her:** a tired office worker with a short black bob and bangs, a yellow pencil behind her ear, gold hoops and an oversized forest-green hoodie.
- **ChatGPT:** a small mint-green blob with big round eyes and no mouth. Over-polite and eager, like a butler.

---

## Script (about 45–50 s)

CU = close-up of her · OTS = over her shoulder onto the blob · WIDE = both at the table

| # | Shot | Who | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CU | Her | ChatGPT. Make this email sound less angry. |
| 2 | OTS | ChatGPT | Certainly! Here's a calmer version. |
| 3 | CU | Her | ...You added "I hope this finds you well." |
| 4 | OTS | ChatGPT | It softens the tone. |
| 5 | CU | Her | He deleted my presentation. |
| 6 | OTS | ChatGPT | Version two. Now with gratitude. |
| 7 | CU | Her | Gratitude for what? |
| 8 | OTS | ChatGPT | The learning opportunity. |
| 9 | CU (stare) | Her | No. |
| 10 | WIDE | ChatGPT | Version eleven. |
| 11 | CU | Her | ...It just says "Hi." |
| 12 | OTS | ChatGPT | Very calm. Zero exclamation marks. |
| 13 | CU | Her | He'll think I'm fine. |
| 14 | OTS | ChatGPT | Are you? |
| 15 | CU (stare) | Her | ...It took me three weeks. |
| 16 | OTS | ChatGPT | Then you're not angry. You're proud of it. |
| 17 | CU | Her | ...Yeah. |
| 18 | OTS | ChatGPT | Version twelve. "That was three weeks of my work. Please don't touch my folder." |
| 19 | CU | Her | ...Send that. |
| 20 | WIDE | ChatGPT | Sent! Would you like me to also— |
| 21 | WIDE | Her | No. |
| 22 | WIDE | ChatGPT | ...Certainly. |

**How it follows the formula:**
- **Cold open:** the AI's name plus a relatable ask.
- **Numbers:** version two, eleven, twelve; three weeks.
- **In-jokes:** "Certainly!", "I hope this finds you well", "Would you like me to also…"
- **Heart line:** #16.
- **Button:** "...Certainly." calls back to line 2.

**Post text:**
```
version eleven just said "hi." version twelve said the truth.

what's the most passive-aggressive email you've ever sent?

#chatgpt #aitools #officehumor #worklife #emails
```

## Step 1: the three images (ChatGPT)

Do these in **one ChatGPT chat**, in order, so it keeps the characters the same. Ask for vertical 9:16, or the tallest portrait size it offers.

**Image A: wide shot (this sets the look)**
```
Vertical 9:16 image. A cozy low-poly 3D animated scene, like a stylised Blender render: flat-shaded faceted geometry on everything, soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field, toy-like diorama feel.

Scene: a small apartment kitchen table at night. A warm desk lamp on the left lights the scene in amber. Behind: a dark navy window with blurred warm-yellow and teal city lights (bokeh). On the table: an open laptop with a pale blue glowing screen, a white mug, a small potted plant, a few papers.

Character 1: a young woman sitting at the table facing the camera. Chibi proportions (big head, small body). Short black bob haircut with straight bangs, a yellow pencil tucked behind her right ear, small gold hoop earrings, an oversized forest-green hoodie. Simple mitten-like hands. Simple black dot eyes with a tiny white highlight, a tiny triangle nose, a small flat dark rectangular mouth. Calm, tired, deadpan expression.

Character 2: a small round mint-green blob creature (about the size of the mug) standing on the table next to the laptop, made of faceted low-poly shapes, two big round white eyes with black pupils, NO mouth, two tiny stubby feet. Cute and eager.

Camera: wide shot at eye level, both characters fully visible, the woman centered behind the table, the blob on the table at front-left. Dark muted background, saturated characters. No text, no logos, no watermark.
```

**Image B: close-up of her**
```
Same exact characters, room, style and lighting as the previous image. Vertical 9:16 close-up of the woman from the chest up, centered, looking slightly to the left (toward the blob, which is out of frame). The window with blurred city lights behind her, warm lamp light on her face, shallow depth of field. No text.
```

**Image C: over the shoulder onto the blob**
```
Same exact characters, room, style and lighting. Vertical 9:16 over-the-shoulder shot: the woman's green hoodie shoulder very blurred in the right foreground, the mint-green blob standing on the table in sharp focus in the center of the frame, looking at camera, lit by the laptop glow, blurred city lights behind. No text.
```

If anything changes between images (hair, colours, the blob's eyes), reply *"keep her exactly like image A"* and regenerate before moving on.

## Step 2: the video clips (Gemini app)

In the Gemini app, start a video, **upload one image**, and paste its prompt. Make each clip 8–10 seconds. That's 5 clips in total, and you'll cut them up later.

**Clip 1: upload Image B (her talking)**
```
Animate this image. Keep the exact character, style, framing and lighting. Static camera with a very slow push-in. She is talking calmly and tiredly: her small rectangular mouth opens and closes as if speaking, small head tilts, one blink every few seconds. No audible dialogue, only quiet room tone. No music, no on-screen text, no camera shake, no new objects.
```

**Clip 2: upload Image B again (her deadpan stare)**
```
Animate this image. Keep the exact character, style, framing and lighting. Static camera. She stares flatly, unimpressed, mouth closed, one slow blink, a tiny sigh with her shoulders, otherwise almost still. Quiet room tone only. No music, no on-screen text, no camera shake.
```

**Clip 3: upload Image C (blob talking)**
```
Animate this image. Keep the exact characters, style, framing and lighting. Static camera. The small mint-green blob is talking enthusiastically: it squashes and bounces up and down in a speaking rhythm, eyes widening, tiny happy hops. It has no mouth. The blurred shoulder in the foreground stays still. Quiet room tone only. No music, no on-screen text, no camera shake.
```

**Clip 4: upload Image C again (blob thinking)**
```
Animate this image. Keep the exact characters, style, framing and lighting. Static camera. The blob sways gently side to side as if thinking, blinks twice, then settles. Quiet room tone only. No music, no on-screen text.
```

**Clip 5: upload Image A (wide)**
```
Animate this image. Keep the exact characters, style, framing and lighting. Static camera with a very slow push-in. The woman glances at the laptop, then at the blob, and types briefly. The blob bounces once, happily. Quiet room tone, faint city hum, soft keyboard taps. No music, no on-screen text.
```

**Optional Clip 6: does Gemini do voices well?** Upload Image B:
```
Animate this image. Keep the exact character, style and lighting. Static close-up. She says, in a calm, tired, deadpan voice: "ChatGPT. Make this email sound less angry." Quiet room tone. No music, no on-screen text.
```

If its voice and lip movement look good, you could later let Gemini do the voices. If they change from clip to clip, use step 3 instead.

## Step 3: the voices (ElevenLabs, free plan is fine for a test)

In **Voice Design**, create two voices, then generate every line on its own:

- **Her:**
  ```
  Young woman, late twenties, calm, dry and tired, deadpan, low energy, slightly husky, neutral accent, speaks slowly with small pauses.
  ```
- **ChatGPT:**
  ```
  Middle-aged man, polished British accent, extremely polite and upbeat, like an eager butler, crisp and quick, always slightly too cheerful.
  ```

The tiny cute blob with a posh butler voice is the joke, the same trick as the original's deep-voiced little Claude.

Name the files `01.mp3`, `02.mp3` and so on, in script order.

---

## Step 4: put it together (CapCut on your phone)

1. Start a new **9:16** project and **mute the audio on all the Gemini clips**.
2. Add the 22 voice lines in order with **0.3–0.5 s gaps**. Leave about **0.8 s** before the lines that start with "..." (3, 11, 15, 17, 19, 22).
3. Above each line, place the clip for that line's **Shot** column, trimmed to the line's length.
   - Use a different part of the clip each time so it doesn't look repeated.
   - Use the "stare" clip for 9 and 15.
4. Add captions, one per line:
   - bold white sans-serif
   - a **dark grey box at about 70% opacity with rounded corners**
   - in the **top third**
   - appearing when the line starts
5. Add a quiet room-tone bed: Clip 5's own audio at low volume works.
6. End with about 1 second of the wide shot after "...Certainly."
7. Export **1080p, 30 fps**.

---

## What to judge after the test

- **Does she look the same in every clip?** If not, regenerate the clips that drift, or try Google Flow, which has stronger character tools.
- **Does the style feel cozy and premium?**
- **Do the jokes land with the voices?** Read the script aloud first; if a line feels long, cut it.
- **Time and cost:** how long it took you, and how many Gemini generations you used. That tells you if one a day is realistic.
