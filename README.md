# @canwebuildthis: teardown and launch plan

Research for launching an Instagram account in the same format as [@canwebuildthis](https://instagram.com/canwebuildthis). There's no code here, only analysis and plans.

All six reels were downloaded and analysed on 13 Oct 2026: frames, shots, colour, audio and transcripts.

## The short version

- **What it is:** a **daily animated mini-sitcom about coding with AI.**
  - A tired developer in a yellow jacket and round glasses works at her desk.
  - Her AI assistants are little low-poly toy characters: an orange **Claude** blob, and from Ep 4 a blue **Codex** cloud.
  - They bicker in short deadpan lines.
- **The numbers** (as of 13 Oct 18:43 UTC):
  - 6 reels in 6 days and **11,383 followers**
  - **971K total views**
  - two hits of **418K** and **366K**, with 10–11% like rates
  - still gaining **about 270 followers and 20,000 views an hour**, measured over 83 minutes that evening; Ep 1 alone brings about 10,500 views an hour
- **The formula:**
  - 45–57 s
  - about 22 lines of about 4 words each, with a cut on every line
  - cold open with the AI's name
  - escalation built on specific numbers
  - the AI's real-life quirk as the joke ("You're absolutely right!", "Quick question", "Done.")
  - one sincere heart line
  - a callback button on a wide shot
- **The look:**
  - low-poly flat-shaded 3D, almost certainly a real Blender render, not AI video
  - one desk set, re-lit each day: night, dusk, golden hour, rain, morning
  - warm light on the characters, cool backgrounds
  - one caption per line in a dark box
  - no music, just voices
- **What won:**
  - real AI behaviour plus a heart line (Ep 1)
  - a rivalry between two AIs (Ep 4)
  - an emotional burnout episode (Ep 5)
- **What lost:** generic tech jokes (Ep 3) and a one-note number loop (Ep 2).
- **Your move:** don't clone it. Take the engine into a lane where you're first, ideally **your language plus a bigger everyday audience (office, students) plus ChatGPT/Gemini characters.**
- **Your tools:**
  - "Veo Omni" is really **Gemini Omni**, used in **Google Flow**
  - **ChatGPT Images 2.5** for designs; Sora is discontinued
  - add **ElevenLabs** for voices (about $6–22/month)
